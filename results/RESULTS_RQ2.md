# Results — RQ2: Contribution of FFR and random-pair AMC (2×2 ablation)

> RQ2: Within RiskProp, how much do future-frame regularization (FFR) and the random-pair adaptive monotonic constraint (AMC) contribute — individually and jointly — to accuracy, low-FAR anticipation, temporal monotonicity, and run-to-run stability?

All numbers below come from `results/rq2/analysis/` (generated by `reeval/re07_infer_rq2.py` and `reeval/re08_analyze_rq2.py`). The RQ2 conclusions use the **internal validation split only**. The official-test rows in Section 7 are supplementary.

## 1. Protocol

**Conditions.** A 2×2 factorial ablation inside RiskProp:

| Condition | FFR | AMC | Loss | Runs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: BCE only | off | off | L_BCE | 3 new (seeds 42/43/44) |
| B: FFR-only | on | off | L_BCE + 0.5 L_FFR | 3 new |
| C: AMC-only | off | on, random pairs | L_BCE + 0.5 L_AMC | 3 new |
| D: full RiskProp | on | on, random pairs | L_BCE + 0.5 L_FFR + 0.5 L_AMC | reused from RQ1 ("RiskProp random") |

D is the RQ1 anchor: full RiskProp with random-offset AMC. It is **not** FixedLag-RiskProp, which belongs to RQ3. D was reused because its configuration is identical. Re-scoring D with the RQ2 pipeline reproduces the RQ1 validation mAP exactly (0.7453 ± 0.0331), so all four conditions are evaluated identically.

**Controls.** The nine new runs use `reeval/re06_train_rq2_ablation.py`. It is a copy of `pipeline/cell34_train_riskprop_cached.py` (v3 matched budget) that only exposes the FFR/AMC switches; `cell34` itself is unchanged. All conditions share:
- the same train/validation split (1,200 / 300 videos, 150 positive + 150 negative in validation; `results/repro/split_manifest_seed42.json`)
- SlowOnly-R50, with 12 causal snippets of 5 frames each at 10 fps and dt = 0.5 s
- SGD with LR 0.01, a step schedule, 50 epochs and batch 2 videos
- λ1 = λ2 = 0.5, W_coll = 8.0, M = 8 AMC pairs, d ~ U(0.1, 0.9), gap = round(d(N−1))

Before training, the split was verified: re-scoring the D seed-42 checkpoint on the rebuilt validation cache matched its original evaluation file on every metric (max |diff| = 0.00005). The training logs confirm that the loss switches worked: A has L_FFR = L_AMC = 0, B has L_AMC = 0, and C has L_FFR = 0 for all 50 epochs.

**Checkpoint rule.** For every condition, among the two checkpoints saved per run (the epoch with the lowest validation loss, and the final epoch), we keep the one with the higher mean validation AP over the 0.5/1.0/1.5 s horizons (ties → lowest-val-loss checkpoint). This is the same rule locked for RQ1/RQ3 (`results/reeval_corrected/locked_config.json`), and it is applied identically to A/B/C/D. *Deviation:* the original RQ2 plan (RQ2_CPV.pdf) selected by validation loss. We switched to validation mAP to stay consistent with RQ1/RQ3 and with mAP as the primary outcome. Chosen checkpoints: `rq2_chosen_checkpoints.json` (A: best/latest/latest, B: latest ×3, C: best/latest/latest, D: latest ×3).

**Metrics.** mAP (mean AP over 0.5/1.0/1.5 s) is the primary metric. Secondary metrics:
- low-FAR metrics at FAR ≤ 0.1: mAUC@0.1, Recall, actual FAR, mTTA, coverage
- temporal metrics on dense causal risk curves
- seed standard deviation

For the temporal metrics, each validation positive is scored at 30 causal 5-frame windows ending at t_event − d, with d = 3.0, 2.9, …, 0.1 s, using the same preprocessing as training. The metrics are computed on these curves (`results/repro/dense_sweep_meta.json`):
- pairwise violation rate: PVR_ε = mean over i < j of 1[a(t_i) − a(t_j) > ε], with ε = 0.01
- average downward step: ADS = mean max(0, a(t) − a(t+1))
- risk-curve jitter: RCJ = mean |a(t+1) − 2a(t) + a(t−1)|

Lower is better for all three. ε was fixed at 0.01 before the temporal metrics were computed; PVR with ε = 0 is reported as a sensitivity check.

**Statistics.** Condition value = mean of its three seeds. Contrasts use a paired, label-stratified bootstrap (B = 2,000, seed 12345) that applies the same resampled validation videos to every condition. A 95% CI that contains 0 is reported as *inconclusive*.

## 5. Findings

1. **FFR accounts for essentially all of the accuracy gain.** Adding FFR raises mAP by about 0.10 without AMC and by 0.09 with it, and mAUC@0.1 by about 0.07 in both cases. All four CIs exclude 0. The gain is largest at the early horizons (AP@1.5s: 0.587 → 0.683, B vs A), and FFR also increases warning time and coverage (mTTA 0.99 → 1.12 s, coverage 45% → 60%). This matches its intended role: propagating collision-frame risk backward in time.
2. **AMC does not change accuracy.** mAP differences are +0.013 without FFR and +0.002 with FFR, and both CIs contain 0. The same holds for mAUC@0.1.
3. **AMC does make risk curves more monotone, but mostly when FFR is absent.** Without FFR, AMC lowers PVR, ADS and RCJ (all CIs < 0). With FFR present, only PVR still improves (−0.017), and the positive interaction terms on PVR/ADS/RCJ mean that AMC's temporal benefit is smaller once FFR is present. This fits the sub-additive (diminishing-returns) hypothesis for the temporal metrics. For mAP there is no evidence of an interaction.
4. **With absolute temporal metrics, FFR looks "worse", but this is partly an amplitude effect** (exploratory, Section 6). Models trained without FFR overfit heavily (final-epoch validation loss 22–32 without FFR vs 6–9 with FFR) and produce low, often almost flat, risk curves. Such curves trivially have few violations and little jitter above ε. With ε = 0, the ordering reverses: every condition with FFR or AMC has fewer violations than A (0.317–0.322 vs 0.353).
5. **Seed stability.** The seed SD of mAP is largest for A (0.063), smallest for B (0.016), and 0.023 / 0.033 for C / D. With three seeds these SDs are imprecise. D's SD is higher than B's, but we do not attribute this to random-pair AMC: in RQ3, fixed-lag pairing gave a low SD at τ = 1.0 s (0.005) but not at τ = 1.5 s (0.030), and not on the official test (`RESULTS_RQ3.md` §5).

## 6. Exploratory (post hoc): curve amplitude behind the temporal metrics

This analysis was not planned in advance; it is computed from the saved dense curves (`results/rq2/raw/dense_rq2/`) to interpret Findings 3–4.

| Condition | Mean positive score | Rise (last 0.3 s − first 0.3 s) | Curves with range < 0.05 | RCJ / curve range (median) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 0.30 | 0.32 | 14% | 0.118 |
| B | 0.52 | 0.36 | 7% | 0.163 |
| C | 0.23 | 0.35 | 22% | 0.097 |
| D | 0.50 | 0.41 | 9% | 0.145 |

Without FFR the curves sit lower and are more often flat, which lowers the absolute PVR/ADS/RCJ. Even after dividing jitter by the curve range, FFR curves remain somewhat rougher, and AMC lowers relative jitter both without FFR (C < A) and with FFR (D < B). The full model shows the largest pre-collision rise.

## 7. Comparison with the RiskProp paper (Table 3, Nexar, "Only Collision Label")

Only the **direction** of each effect is comparable. The paper reports a single run on the official Nexar test set with λ1 = 1.5, λ2 = 1.1, LR 0.002, batch 64 and frame-level sequences. Ours are three seeds on the internal validation split with the settings in Section 1.

| Contrast | Paper Δ mAP | Ours Δ mAP [95% CI] | Paper Δ mAUC@0.1 | Ours Δ mAUC@0.1 [95% CI] | Agreement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FFR, AMC off (B − A) | +0.073 | +0.103 [0.062, 0.144] | +0.155 | +0.070 [0.008, 0.143] | agrees |
| AMC, FFR off (C − A) | +0.004 | +0.013 [−0.021, 0.044] | +0.004 | +0.014 [−0.025, 0.051] | not confirmed (both effects are small) |
| FFR, AMC on (D − C) | +0.085 | +0.092 [0.054, 0.130] | +0.170 | +0.065 [0.005, 0.135] | agrees |
| AMC, FFR on (D − B) | +0.016 | +0.002 [−0.026, 0.028] | +0.019 | +0.010 [−0.045, 0.068] | not confirmed |
| Interaction | +0.012 | −0.011 [−0.050, 0.031] | +0.015 | −0.005 [−0.067, 0.059] | not confirmed |

Our results reproduce the paper's main message that FFR is the dominant component. The paper's small additional gain from AMC (+0.016 mAP on top of FFR) is within our uncertainty and could not be confirmed.

The test set shows the same pattern as validation:
- FFR raises mAP by 0.12 without AMC, CI [0.100, 0.147], and by 0.10 with AMC, CI [0.078, 0.123].

## 9. Deviations and limitations

- **Checkpoint rule:** validation mAP instead of the validation loss named in RQ2_CPV.pdf, applied identically to all conditions and to RQ1/RQ3. Only two checkpoints were saved per run (lowest validation loss, final epoch), so "best by mAP" means the better of these two, not the best of all 50 epochs.
- **Temporal metrics:** the proposal names PVR/ADS/RCJ without giving formulas or ε. The definitions and ε = 0.01 above were fixed before the temporal metrics were computed. The amplitude analysis (Section 6) is post hoc.
- **Three seeds only:** seed SDs are imprecise, and the bootstrap CIs resample videos, not seeds.
- **Setup differs from the paper:** λ, learning rate, batch size and snippet-level sequences all differ, so absolute scores are not comparable with the paper. W_coll = 8.0 is the value actually used (the header of `cell32`'s docstring still mentions 5.0, but its "v2 UPDATE" block and the code use 8.0).
- **Cost:** the nine new RQ2 runs took about 11.3 GPU-hours on one RTX 4080 (about 90 s per epoch).

## 10. Ghi chú nội bộ để họp nhóm (xóa mục này trước khi nộp)

Những điểm cần partner và cả nhóm xem xét, thống nhất trước khi viết bản cuối:

1. **Luật chọn checkpoint.** Chọn theo val mAP, nhưng chỉ giữa 2 checkpoint đã lưu (epoch có val_loss thấp nhất và epoch cuối), giống RQ1/RQ3. Đây là deviation so với `RQ2_CPV.pdf` gốc (bản đó chọn theo val_loss). Cách ghi ở mục 1 và mục 9 đã đủ rõ chưa?
2. **RQ2 trên official test (mục 8).** Protocol của partner ghi RQ2 chỉ kết luận từ val. Nhóm giữ mục 8 ở phụ lục (ghi rõ exploratory, không dùng test để chọn gì) hay bỏ hẳn?
3. **Diễn giải chỉ số temporal.** Với ε = 0.01, FFR có PVR/ADS/RCJ "xấu hơn". Với ε = 0 thì ngược lại, FFR ít vi phạm hơn A. Phân tích biên độ đường risk (mục 6) là làm thêm sau khi có kết quả (post hoc). Đưa mục 6 vào bài chính hay phụ lục?
4. **Interaction.** Trên val, interaction về mAP không có ý nghĩa. Interaction trên temporal thì dương (AMC ít tác dụng hơn khi đã có FFR). Dùng từ "sub-additive / diminishing returns" cho phần temporal được không?
5. **Wcoll trong docstring `cell32` ghi 5.0**, còn code chạy 8.0. Sửa comment trong `cell32` (không đổi kết quả) hay chỉ ghi chú trong báo cáo như hiện tại?
6. **So với paper.** Tác dụng của AMC trên accuracy "not confirmed". Paper cũng chỉ thấy AMC tăng +0.004 và +0.016. Viết "not confirmed" hay "consistent with a small effect that our design cannot resolve"?
7. **Độ dao động giữa seed.** SD mAP của D (0.033) lớn hơn B (0.016). Trước đây mình nối điều này với RQ3 (fixed-lag τ1.0 có SD 0.005). Nhưng run sensitivity τ = 1.5 s có SD 0.030, và trên official test SD của fixed và random gần bằng nhau, nên Finding 5 đã được sửa: không quy độ dao động cho AMC random nữa (xem `RESULTS_RQ3.md` §5). Đồng ý không?
8. **Trên test, mAUC@0.1 có interaction âm có ý nghĩa** (−0.074, CI [−0.112, −0.032]). Kết quả này khác val (không có ý nghĩa) và khác paper (+0.015). Có bàn trong Discussion không, hay chỉ để trong bảng phụ lục?
